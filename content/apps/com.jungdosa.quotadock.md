QuotaDock sits in a corner of the Windows desktop and shows how much of your Claude Code, OpenAI Codex and Google Antigravity session and weekly limits is left, with a countdown to each reset. It uses the sign-ins those tools already have, so there is nothing to paste and no telemetry. It comes in normal, compact and nano sizes and in 12 languages. Built with Fyne; Windows 10 22H2&#43; and 11 for now, macOS planned.
