A clean, fast, distraction-free way to read and study the Bible.

• Distraction-free reading — the World English Bible (including a Catholic edition), the Berean Standard Bible, and the New King James Version — words of Christ in red, light and dark themes.

• AI study — select any passage and have it explained, its context unpacked, or its translation discussed. Bring your own AI key; entirely optional.

• Share — any verse as a clean, typeset image card, or as text with a Bluebook-style citation.

• Notes from friends — send a verse with a short note carried inside the link. Public-domain passages open with the note in the app or browser; licensed-translation links show the note and a licensing notice on the web, with a route into the app. No account, no server.

• Narration — complete human-recorded audio, with read-along verse highlighting and on-device read-aloud. Audio keeps playing with the screen locked.

Free and open source. No ads, no accounts, no tracking.
