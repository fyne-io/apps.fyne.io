Froggy is a nostalgic, single-player Frogger clone built with Fyne.
Hop across five lanes of traffic, over the median, then ride the logs and turtles across the river to the five home bays at the top.
Fill every bay to clear the level — the next one runs faster and more turtles dive, and the game ends when your frogs run out.
