A voxel world built with Fyne, inspired by the blocky adventure games you already know.
It generates a landscape of hills, trees and lakes under a broken layer of cloud and drops you into the middle of it to walk around, look about with the mouse and dig blocks out.

The world is drawn on the graphics card by a fragment shader, with sunlight, shadows, fog and see-through water, and every block wears a pixelated 16x16 texture.
A software renderer is included as well for machines without a suitable graphics card.
